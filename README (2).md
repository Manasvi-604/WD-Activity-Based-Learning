## Case Study 1: Online Food Ordering Website

**Objective:** Develop a simple website that displays food items and allows users to select dishes to order.

**Features:**
- Navigation links for the home page and food items.
- Menu cards for chicken biryani and butter chicken masala.
- Prices and ratings displayed for each item.
- “Order Now” buttons for ordering actions.

**Outcome:** The website presents a clear food menu and demonstrates how product details and order actions can be organized on a web page.

## Case Study 2: Sweet Bakery Website

**Objective:** Create a bakery website to showcase cakes, pastries, cookies, and special offers.

**Features:**
- Product cards with item names and prices.
- A special offer section advertising a discount on cakes.
- A custom cake designs section.
- “Order Now” buttons for the displayed products.

**Outcome:** The website provides a simple online catalogue that helps customers browse bakery products and notice promotions.

## Case Study 3: Yoga and Meditation Website

**Objective:** Build a website that introduces yoga classes, meditation, wellness tips, schedules, and membership plans.

**Features:**
- Sections for yoga poses, meditation, and wellness tips.
- Morning yoga and evening meditation schedules.
- Weekly and monthly membership prices.
- “Learn More,” “Read Tips,” and “Join Now” buttons.

**Outcome:** The website organizes wellness information and class details in one place, helping visitors explore activities and membership options.
